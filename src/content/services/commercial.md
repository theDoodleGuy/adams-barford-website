## Building work that doesn't stop your business

When you run a business, the building work isn't the point. Keeping customers served and staff working is. So we plan commercial jobs around how you operate. That can mean phasing the work room by room, doing the noisy and dusty parts out of hours or at weekends, and keeping entrances, fire exits and toilets clear the whole time.

## What we do

We refurbish and fit out offices, and carry out alterations, extensions and layout changes to business premises across Southampton, the Waterside and the New Forest. Our team is used to working in high-end spaces where the finish has to be right.

## One firm, one point of contact

Bigger contractors can be expensive, and you often deal with someone different every week. With us, you deal with the same family-run team from the first meeting to completion. There's one person accountable for the programme and the budget, and you'll get straight answers when you ask how things are going.

## Clear prices and programmes

You'll get a clear, itemised quote and a written programme before we start, so you can plan around it. If you ask for changes once we're on site, we'll price them and agree them with you in writing before we do the work. At the end, we hand over the certificates and paperwork you need for your records and your landlord or insurer.

## What it typically costs

*Guidance only. Commercial jobs vary widely with the building, the specification and when the work can happen.*

- **Office refurbishment (decoration, flooring, lighting, small layout changes):** roughly £400 to £1,000 per square metre [confirm with owner]
- **Smaller alterations and fit-out work:** usually from around £10,000 [confirm with owner]

Out-of-hours working adds cost, so we'll show you the options and let you choose.

## How long it takes

A small office refurbishment can take 2 to 6 weeks. We'll agree a programme with you before we start and stick to it [confirm with owner].
