## More room without moving

Most of the extensions we build are for people who like where they live but have run out of space. A kitchen the whole family fits in. A downstairs bedroom for an older parent. A home office that isn't the dining table. We've been building them across Hythe, the Waterside, Southampton and the New Forest since 1995.

## Building around you while you live there

Nearly everyone we build for stays at home while we work, so we plan for it from day one. We agree which rooms you'll lose and when. We keep your kitchen running for as long as we can, and we break through into the house as late in the job as possible. Floors are covered, the site is tidied at the end of every day, and you'll know who's arriving each morning. It's the same faces from the first visit to handover.

## Drawings, planning and regulations

We've built up good connections with local planners and trades over the years. If you already have plans, we'll build from them. If you don't, we can introduce you to a designer we work with [confirm with owner].

We'll tell you early on whether your extension is likely to need planning permission. That matters round here. Inside the New Forest National Park the rules are stricter, and the National Park Authority looks closely at size and design. Building Regulations approval and inspections are part of the job, and we'll arrange them.

## What it typically costs

*Guidance only. Every home is different, and we'll give you a clear, itemised quote after we've visited.*

- **Single-storey extension:** roughly £2,000 to £3,000 per square metre. A typical 20 to 25 m² kitchen extension often comes in between £50,000 and £90,000 [confirm with owner]
- **Two-storey extension:** roughly £1,800 to £2,800 per square metre across both floors [confirm with owner]

The kitchen, the glazing (bifolds and roof lanterns add up quickly) and the level of finish make the biggest difference. Design, planning and Building Regulations fees are usually on top. We'll be clear about what's included before you commit.

## How long it takes

As a rough guide, a single-storey extension takes 10 to 16 weeks on site once the approvals are in place [confirm with owner]. You'll get a timeline for your project with the quote, and we'll tell you straight away if anything changes.
