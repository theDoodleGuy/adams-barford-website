## Making a house work for the way you live now

Some of our favourite jobs don't add a single square metre. They take a house that's tired, dark or awkward and make it work. That might be knocking a kitchen and dining room into one, moving a bathroom, or stripping a bungalow back and starting again inside. We've specialised in this kind of work since 1995, and a lot of it comes from people we've built for before.

## Older homes

Plenty of homes round here are older than they look. When we renovate them, we try to keep what makes them special: original brickwork, sash windows, cornicing and fireplaces. Where something can't be saved, we'll replace it with materials that suit the house rather than fight it. If your home is listed or in a conservation area, we'll check what consent you need before any work starts.

## Living in it, or moving out

Most people stay at home while we work, and we plan the job around that. We'll phase the work so you always have somewhere to cook and wash, and we tidy up every day. For a whole-house refurbishment, it can sometimes be quicker, cheaper and calmer to move out for a few weeks. If we think that's the case, we'll say so at the start.

## When the walls hold surprises

Older houses hide things: rotten joists, old wiring, a beam that isn't where the drawings say. We'll warn you about likely risks before we start. If we find something, we'll show you, explain the options and agree any extra cost with you before we do the work. No surprises on the final bill.

- **Knocking through, with a new steel beam:** usually from around £8,000 [confirm with owner]

## How long it takes

A kitchen or layout change is often 4 to 8 weeks. A full refurbishment can be 3 to 6 months [confirm with owner]. We'll give you a timeline with the quote.
