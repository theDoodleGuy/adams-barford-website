## Oak suits the New Forest

Few things look as right here as a solid oak frame. It weathers to a soft silver, sits well next to brick, flint and thatch, and lasts for generations. We build oak porches, garages, car ports, garden rooms, home offices and oak-framed extensions for homes across the Forest, the Waterside and beyond.

## The frame and everything around it

An oak frame is only part of the job. It also needs a level base or foundations, a roof, glazing, and often power and heating. Some oak firms supply the frame and leave the rest to you. We handle the whole project, so one team is responsible for the groundworks, the frame going up, and the finishing touches. ## What to expect from green oak

Most oak frames are made from green oak, which is freshly cut and still drying out. As it dries over the first few years, it moves slightly and small splits called shakes appear along the grain. That's normal, and it doesn't weaken the frame. It's part of the character. We'll explain all of this before you choose, so nothing comes as a surprise.

## Do you need planning?

Many garden buildings and porches fall under permitted development, so they don't need planning permission. The rules are tighter in the New Forest National Park and in conservation areas, for listed homes, and for anything in front of the house. A garden room you plan to sleep in is treated differently too. We'll check for your property at the first visit.
