## Building a new home, start to finish

We build one-off houses for people who own a plot, or who want to replace an existing house with something better. It's the biggest project most people will ever take on, and the one where knowing your builder matters most. You'll deal with the same family from the first site meeting until we hand you the keys, and we'll still be around afterwards.

## One builder from the ground up

We take care of the whole build: clearing and demolition if needed, groundworks, drainage, the structure, roof, windows, first and second fix, and the finishing. We bring in trusted local trades we've worked with for years, so you have one point of contact and one firm accountable for all of it.

We'll work with your architect and engineer, or we can introduce you to people we trust [confirm with owner]. Getting the builder involved before the drawings are finished often saves money, because we can flag details that are costly to build.

## Warm, efficient homes

Every new home has to meet current Building Regulations for insulation, airtightness and energy use, and we build to them as standard. If you want to go further, we'll talk you through the options, such as air source heat pumps, solar panels and better glazing, and give you honest costs so you can decide what's worth it.

## Replacement homes in the New Forest

If your plot is inside the New Forest National Park, the National Park Authority has its own rules on how large a replacement house can be and how it should look. Your architect will lead on the planning application, and we'll make sure what gets approved can be built well and within budget.

## What it typically costs

*Guidance only. Every plot and design is different, and we'll give you a clear, itemised quote from your drawings.*

- **Build cost:** roughly £2,200 to £3,500 per square metre, depending on design and specification [confirm with owner]
- So a 150 m² family home would often cost somewhere between £330,000 and £525,000 to build [confirm with owner]

That's the build only. Land, design fees, planning, surveys, connections and VAT treatment all need budgeting for separately. We'll help you see the full picture.

## How long it takes

Most one-off homes take 9 to 15 months on site, once planning and Building Regulations are approved [confirm with owner].
