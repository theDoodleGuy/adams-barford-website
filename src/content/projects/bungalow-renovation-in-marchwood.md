## The brief

A retired couple had bought a 1960s bungalow to be closer to family. It had a warren of small rooms, single glazing and a bathroom that wouldn't work for them in the years ahead. They wanted it opened up, warmer and easier to live in.

## The challenge

Once we lifted the floors, we found rotten joists in two rooms and wiring that needed replacing throughout. The couple were renting nearby, so the budget and timeline mattered.

## How we did it

We showed them the joists the same day, explained the options and agreed the extra cost in writing before carrying on. We removed two internal walls with new steel beams to create an open kitchen and living space. We also rewired and replumbed, insulated the loft and floors, fitted new windows and built a level-access shower room.

## The result

A warm, bright home with a kitchen that looks out over the garden. The final bill matched the agreed quote plus the one agreed change.
