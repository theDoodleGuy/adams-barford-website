## The brief

The owner of a brick cottage near Lyndhurst worked from home and needed a proper office. They also wanted cover for two cars, without anything looking modern or out of place next to the house.

## The challenge

The cottage sits in a conservation area within the National Park, so the design needed planning permission and had to suit the setting. The site also sloped towards the lane.

## How we did it

We worked with the client's designer on a green oak frame with a clay-tiled roof to match the cottage. Planning was approved first time. We levelled the site with a new base and retaining wall, then raised the frame in two days. The office above has insulation, heating, power and data, reached by an oak staircase.

## The result

A double car port with a warm, quiet office in the roof space. The oak has already started to soften towards silver.
