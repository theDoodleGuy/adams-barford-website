## The brief

A family of five had outgrown a narrow 1970s galley kitchen. They wanted one room where they could cook, eat and keep an eye on the children in the garden, without moving away from their schools and friends.

## The challenge

They needed to stay at home for the whole build. The only way to the back garden was a narrow side path shared with a neighbour, and the new opening meant a large steel beam across the back of the house.

## How we did it

We built the extension shell first and kept the old kitchen working until the shell was watertight. Only then did we knock through, over a single planned week. A temporary kitchen went into the dining room for that stretch. We agreed delivery times with the neighbour and kept the shared path clear every evening.

## The result

A bright 24 m² kitchen and family room with roof lanterns and bifold doors onto the garden. The family lost the use of their kitchen for nine days in total.
