## The brief

A growing firm of 20 staff needed its tired office brought up to date: a new layout with meeting rooms, a kitchen area, new lighting and flooring throughout.

## The challenge

The business couldn't close, and clients visited most days. Noisy, dusty work had to happen when nobody was there, and the fire exits had to stay clear at all times.

## How we did it

We split the office into three phases and moved staff around as each part was finished. Stud walls, electrics and anything noisy happened in the evenings and at weekends. Decorating and carpets went in at the very end. We agreed a written programme up front and sent the owner a short update at the end of each week.

## The result

Two meeting rooms, a bright kitchen and breakout area, and a calmer open-plan office. It was finished on programme without a single lost working day.
