## The brief

The owners had lived in a draughty 1950s house for years and wanted to replace it with a warm, low-running-cost home for retirement, with room for visiting grandchildren.

## The challenge

The plot is inside the New Forest National Park, so the replacement house had to respect strict limits on size and appearance. The access lane was narrow and unmade, which ruled out large deliveries on wet days.

## How we did it

We joined the project early, while the architect was preparing the planning application. That let us suggest details that kept the approved design affordable to build. After demolition, we built a highly insulated, airtight house with an air source heat pump and solar panels. Deliveries were planned around the weather and the neighbours, and the same site team ran the job from groundworks to handover.

## The result

A four-bedroom home in brick and timber cladding that sits comfortably in its setting, with very low energy bills in its first winter.
