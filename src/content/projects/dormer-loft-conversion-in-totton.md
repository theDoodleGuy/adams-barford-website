## The brief

A couple in a 1930s semi had a new baby on the way and no spare room. They loved the street and didn't want to move, so they asked whether the loft could become a main bedroom with its own shower room.

## The challenge

The existing roof didn't have enough head height for a usable room. The new staircase also had to fit on a small landing without taking space from the bedroom below.

## How we did it

We worked with their designer on a rear dormer, which gave full head height across most of the room. Most of the work happened from scaffolding at the back of the house, so the family carried on as normal. We only opened up the landing in the last three weeks, when we fitted a winding staircase that tucked over the existing stairs.

## The result

A quiet main bedroom with an en-suite, built-in storage under the eaves and views over the rooftops towards the water.
