As a rough guide, a single-storey extension in the New Forest and Southampton area costs around £2,000 to £3,000 per square metre to build [confirm with owner]. A typical 20 to 25 m² kitchen extension often comes in between £50,000 and £90,000 [confirm with owner]. The kitchen, the amount of glazing and the level of finish make the biggest difference. Design, planning and Building Regulations fees are usually extra. These figures are guidance only. Adams & Barford gives every customer a clear, itemised quote after visiting the property.
