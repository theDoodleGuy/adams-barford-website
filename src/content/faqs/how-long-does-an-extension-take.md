question: How long does an extension take?
category: process
order: 1
placeholder: true
---

A single-storey extension usually takes 10 to 16 weeks on site [confirm with owner]. Before that, allow time for drawings (often 3 to 6 weeks) and, if you need planning permission, around 8 weeks for the council or National Park Authority to decide once your application is validated. Loft conversions are typically 8 to 12 weeks on site and garage conversions 4 to 8 weeks [confirm with owner]. We'll give you a timeline for your project with the quote, and tell you straight away if anything changes.
