category: planning

For most extensions, conversions and new homes, you'll need proper drawings for planning and Building Regulations. They can come from an architect or an architectural designer. If you already have one, we're happy to build from their plans. If you don't, we can introduce you to designers we work with [confirm with owner]. Smaller jobs, such as a garden room or knocking through a wall, may only need a structural engineer's calculations. We'll tell you what your project needs at the first visit.
