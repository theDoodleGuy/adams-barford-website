Adams & Barford has been building in Hythe since 1995. The business was started by Paul Adams and Lyndsey Barford and is still family-run today. Almost all of our work has come from word of mouth: people we've built for before, and their friends and neighbours. You'll see the same family and the same faces from the first visit to handover.
