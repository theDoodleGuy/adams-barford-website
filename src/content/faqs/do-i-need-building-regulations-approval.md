---
question: Do I need Building Regulations approval?
category: planning
order: 2
placeholder: true
---

For almost all extensions, loft and garage conversions, structural changes and new homes, yes. Building Regulations are separate from planning permission. They cover safety and performance: structure, fire safety, insulation, drainage and ventilation. A building control inspector, from the council or an approved private provider, checks the work at key stages and issues a completion certificate at the end. We arrange this as part of the job and hand you the certificate at handover.
