question: Can we live at home while the work is done?
category: process
order: 2
placeholder: true
---

Usually, yes. Most of our customers stay at home, and building around families who live in the house is what we do best. We plan the work so you keep a kitchen and bathroom for as long as possible, and we tell you in advance when you won't. We protect floors, tidy up every day and keep the site safe for children and pets. For a whole-house refurbishment, moving out for a few weeks can sometimes be quicker and calmer. If so, we'll say so at the start.
