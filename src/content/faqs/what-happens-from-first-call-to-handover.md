question: What happens from first call to handover?
category: process
order: 3
placeholder: true
---

First, we have a short chat about what you have in mind. Then we visit your home to look at the space and talk through ideas. Once drawings are ready, you get a clear, itemised quote. We help with planning and Building Regulations, agree a start date, and build with the same family team throughout, keeping you updated as we go. At the end, we walk through everything with you, fix any snags and hand over the certificates.
