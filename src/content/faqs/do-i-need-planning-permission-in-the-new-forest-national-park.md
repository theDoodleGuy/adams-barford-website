---
question: Do I need planning permission in the New Forest National Park?
category: planning
order: 1
placeholder: true
---

Often, yes. Inside the National Park, planning is handled by the New Forest National Park Authority, not the district council, and the rules are stricter. Permitted development rights are more limited, some areas have Article 4 directions or are conservation areas, and the Authority also limits how much homes outside the defined villages can be extended over time. Outside the Park, New Forest District Council or Southampton City Council deals with applications. We'll check what applies to your property at the first visit, and your designer can confirm before you apply.
